## 1. Overview and Audit Standard

This log documents every material correction enacted between the unverified Stage 2 / reviewed_v1 assessments and this critical re-audit (reviewed_v2.1). In accordance with rigorous scientific reproducibility standards, this audit enforces an explicit **7-level verification hierarchy**:

1. **Level 1 — Schema / File Validation:** Validates file formatting, CSV quoting, delimiter boundaries, and parsing syntax. *(Structural integrity only; does NOT imply factual truth).*
2. **Level 2 — URL & DOI Resolution:** Validates that web endpoints, DOIs, or legal portal links resolve to active HTTP servers without 404/DNS errors.
3. **Level 3 — Bibliographic Verification:** Cross-references author names, titles, publication dates, volume/issue numbers, and issuing bodies against authoritative indexes (Crossref, PubMed, Google Scholar, LiveLaw, eCourts, PIB).
4. **Level 4 — Abstract-Only Review:** Synthesizes explicitly declared abstracts when full text is paywalled or restricted. Prohibits claiming methodology or findings beyond the abstract.
5. **Level 5 — Full-Text Review:** In-depth examination of the complete source manuscript, judgment order, or technical report.
6. **Level 6 — Independent Verification of Specific Claim:** Primary record or accredited legal reporter independently examined to verify that the text explicitly asserts the specific empirical proposition attributed to it.
7. **Level 7 — Unresolved / Conflicting Evidence:** Flags discrepancies where secondary accounts, docket listings, or author claims diverge or remain unconfirmed.
