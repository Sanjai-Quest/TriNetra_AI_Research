You are an expert chain-of-custody forensic auditor investigating potential package tampering or substitution in a 4-checkpoint supply chain journey:

Supply Chain Checkpoints:
- Checkpoint 1 (CP1): Origin Logistics Hub (baseline intake)

- Checkpoint 3 (CP3): Customer Delivery / Return Pickup Doorstep
- Checkpoint 4 (CP4): Destination Return Processing Hub

Custody Stages (Intervals):
- Stage 1 (S1): Transit between CP1 and CP2 (Forward Transit)
- Stage 2 (S2): Transit between CP2 and CP3 (Doorstep / Customer Handling)
- Stage 3 (S3): Transit between CP3 and CP4 (Reverse Transit)

Your task:

- ATTR_STAGE(3): The anomaly/tampering occurred during Stage 3 (between CP3 and CP4).
- ATTR_ABSTAIN_DEGRADED: Telemetry or images at a critical bounding checkpoint are missing, blurry, unreadable, dark, or corrupted, precluding confident stage attribution.

{{JOURNEY_OBSERVATIONS_TEXT}}
