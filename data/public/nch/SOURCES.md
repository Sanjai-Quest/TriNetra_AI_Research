# National Consumer Helpline (NCH) Public Parliamentary Datasets

This directory contains authentic public statistical tables released by the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution, Government of India, in responses to Unstarred Questions in the Rajya Sabha (Council of States), Parliament of India.

These tables represent **macro-aggregate statistics** on national consumer grievances, e-commerce dispute volumes, and judicial pendency across Indian consumer commissions. They do **not** contain row-level dispute records, customer PII, package serials, or physical telemetry, and must never be cited as empirical ground truth for physical custody attribution.

## Licensing and Usage Notice
- **Source:** Parliament of India (Rajya Sabha Questions & Answers / data.gov.in / Ministry of Consumer Affairs).
- **Status:** Public Government Data / Parliamentary Public Record (India).
- **Permitted Use:** Macro-level domain motivation and industry problem sizing.
- **Prohibited Use:** Must not be used to synthetically validate granular physical detection algorithms or misapplied as unit-level ground truth.
